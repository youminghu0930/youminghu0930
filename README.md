## About me

I’m Youming. My work connects computer vision, geometry, and movement analysis. I’m interested in making complex motion easier to reconstruct, inspect, and understand.

My current work uses golf swings to explore reliable tracking, 3D reconstruction, and interpretable motion measurements from RGB video.

## Work & achievements

Selected work documented in my public portfolio.

Developed work around golf-specific pose estimation, golfer and club detection, synthetic motion blur, and trajectory postprocessing.
